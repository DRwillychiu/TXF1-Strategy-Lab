## 7. Lessons

**L-v1.10.0-1**: When a strategy's alpha is 96%+ attributable to a single
filter, that filter is not optional infrastructure — it is the strategy's
core thesis. Design reviews should identify such components and protect
them from removal experiments.

**L-v1.10.0-2**: "Complete all optimization stages before comparing" is
the correct scientific method. Premature conclusions based on partial
optimization (Stage 1 only) could have been wrong in either direction.
The user's insistence on full completion was the right call.

**L-v1.10.0-3**: Monotonic optimization curves (no extremum) within a
physically calibrated parameter range are a structural signal, not a
parameter range problem. They indicate the hypothesis itself lacks edge
in that regime.
