## 1. Problem

At index level 40K-48K, ATR-based initial stop loss (P3) produces 500-1000 pt stops.
ATR scales non-linearly with index level — the higher the index, the wider the stop.
In choppy markets, this means painful 100K-200K TWD single-trade losses on false breakouts.

**Goal**: Add a tighter SL mechanism that caps stop distance at high index levels,
without interfering at low index levels where ATR is already reasonable.

---

## 2. Solution: Fixed Percentage Cap (SL_Pct)

Added a third leg to the existing P3 triple-layered initial stop:

| Leg | Formula | Role |
|-----|---------|------|
| Leg 1 | 45M ATR × SL_Multiplier | Intraday volatility stop |
| Leg 2 | Daily ATR × Daily_Cap_Multiplier | Daily volatility ceiling |
| **Leg 3 (NEW)** | **EntryPrice × SL_Pct / 100** | **Percentage cap (V3.1)** |

`v_Frozen_SL = MaxList(Leg1, MaxList(Leg2, Leg3))` — MaxList picks the TIGHTEST (highest price for long).

**Key property**: Percentage scales linearly with index. At high index (44K), 0.5% = 220 pts cap.
At low index (12K), 0.5% = 60 pts — but ATR is already tighter there, so Leg 3 never binds.

**Backward compatible**: `SL_Pct = 0` disables Leg 3 entirely → exact V3.0 behavior.

## 5. Winner: SL_Pct = 0.5%

### 5.1 Mechanism

At 44K index level: stop distance = 44000 × 0.5% = 220 pts (vs ATR's 500+ pts).

- Kills 4 small winners (Win 174 → 170, -2.3%)
- 310 losing trades each lose 606 TWD less on average → ~188K total saved
- Fast re-entry after tight stop generates additional winners
- Gross Profit actually INCREASES +81K (net of the killed winners)
- Net improvement: **+57K (+3.1%)**

## 7. Abandoned Approach: L1_c (Daily Drawdown)

Before SL_Pct, we tried L1_c (10-day max close-to-close drawdown × ratio).
Diagnostic log proved L1_c scales WITH volatility (same direction as ATR).
At Ratio=0.7, L1_c only bound 8.7% of trades (41/469).
At index >= 20K, L1_c × 0.7 was 2-8x WIDER than ATR × 1.5.

**Conclusion**: L1_c does not solve the problem. Reverted to V3.0 and took the SL_Pct approach instead.
